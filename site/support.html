Support

Something looks wrong, or have an idea? Here is how to get help.

A site looks broken

Press Alt+Shift+D or click the toolbar button to flip that site back. Shadeflip only changes sites you choose, so nothing else is affected.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. Include the site address, your browser and version, and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Why do images look normal on a dark site? Shadeflip darkens pages by inverting colours, then flips images and videos back so they keep their real colours.

Can I make every site dark? Yes. In the extension's options, set the default to Dark everywhere, then flip individual sites back to light.

Does it work on subdomains? Each exact hostname has its own setting, so mail.example.com and example.com are set separately.
