Privacy policy

Shadeflip collects nothing. This page explains what that means.

What Shadeflip stores

The only data Shadeflip keeps is your settings: the default mode and the list of sites you have set to dark or light. These are saved in your browser's extension storage on your device. If you use your browser's own sync feature, your browser may sync these settings between your devices; Shadeflip itself never sends them anywhere.

What Shadeflip does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • It does not track the pages you visit or read page content beyond checking a page's background brightness, which happens locally.
  • It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or ads.

Permissions

Shadeflip needs access to the pages you visit so it can apply dark or light styling to them, and storage access to remember your settings. It uses these only for that purpose.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Shadeflip deletes its stored settings. You can also clear any site from the list in the extension's options.

This website

shadeflip.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log standard request data as the host; see GitHub's privacy statement.
