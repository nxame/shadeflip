Dark or light, one site at a time.

Shadeflip is a tiny browser extension. Click it and the site you are on flips dark or light. It remembers that choice for that site only.

news.exampleToo bright at night? One click.
news.exampleNow dark, and it stays that way.

How it works

  • Click the toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+D, to flip the current site.
  • Each site keeps its own setting. Nothing else changes.
  • Pages that are already dark or light are left alone.
  • Optionally make every site dark or light by default, and override per site.

Why another one

Most dark mode extensions switch every site at once, break some layouts, and ask for donations. Shadeflip does one thing, is a few kilobytes, and never asks for anything.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no network requests. Your per-site choices stay in your browser. Read the privacy policy.
